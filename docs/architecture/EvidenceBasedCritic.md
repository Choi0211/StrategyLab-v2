# Evidence-Based Critic

Sprint 118 grounds strategy critique in the current StrategySpec, dataset
quality, backtest result, and walk-forward validation.

Qualitative structure findings may be generated from the strategy itself, but
numeric claims require a backtest or validation evidence reference. The critic
does not fabricate MDD, Sharpe, or win-rate values when the corresponding result
is absent.

Findings are advisory only and do not promote Champions or alter deployment.
